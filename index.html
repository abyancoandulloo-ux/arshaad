About Us

Your Trusted Driving School in Mauritius

At Arshaad Driving Academy, we transform beginners into skilled, safety‑conscious drivers. We are proud to be one of the leading auto écoles in Mauritius, offering both manual and automatic transmission training. We don't just teach you to pass the test – we teach you to drive for life.

Where Great Drivers Are Made

With over a decade of experience, we provide high‑quality professional driving lessons across Mauritius. Our patient, experienced instructors use modern teaching techniques to help you master the road – whether you prefer a manual gearbox or the ease of an automatic.

We go beyond the test. We teach post‑licence driving so you feel confident on any road. You'll also learn basic car maintenance – checking engine oil, coolant/water, tyre pressure – and how to prevent car damage through smart driving habits.
